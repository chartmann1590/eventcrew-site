How it works

Three steps, and the friends joining you never have to download anything.

  1. 1

    Start an event

    The leader opens the EventCrew app, names the event, sets when it ends and shares the invite link in any group chat.

  2. 2

    Friends tap the link

    It opens in their phone's browser. They pick a nickname and allow location. No account, no email, no install. By default the leader approves each person.

  3. 3

    Stay together

    Everyone sees the crew on a live map, chats with the group, shares photos and heads for the leader's meetup pin when it's time to regroup.

Privacy first

Here for the event. Gone after.

EventCrew is built to forget. Nothing about your night is kept once it's over.

Deleted for good when it ends

When the leader taps End, or the end time passes, the event's whole database is wiped in one step: locations, chat, photos and nicknames. No backups, no archive, no "recently deleted".

Leave any time

Tap Leave and your location, messages and photos are deleted from the server right away, and the event is wiped from your browser.

Encrypted on your phone

Nicknames, chat, photos, locations and the meetup pin are encrypted on your device. The key lives only in the invite link, so our server only ever holds scrambled data.

No accounts

No email, phone number or real name. Just a nickname for this one event.

Location only during the event

Only your latest spot is kept, never a trail. Choose exact or approximate (about 100 m), or pause sharing whenever you like.

Clean photos, no trackers

Photos are re-drawn on your phone to strip hidden location data before they're shared. The friends' web app has no analytics.

FAQ

Do my friends need the app?

No. Only the person who starts the event uses the Android app. Everyone else taps the invite link and joins in their phone's browser, on Android or iPhone. They can add it to their home screen if they want notifications.

What happens to my data?

It exists only while the event is running. Content you share (nickname, chat, photos, location) is encrypted on your phone before it's sent. When the event ends, everything about it is permanently deleted from the server in one step, photos included. If you leave early, your own location, messages and photos are deleted right away. We don't sell data, and there are no accounts to keep. The Privacy Policy has the details.

Can I leave whenever I want?

Yes, any time, with one tap. Leaving deletes your location, messages and photos from the event immediately.

Who can join my event?

Only people with your invite link, and by default you approve each one before they see anything. You can remove people, make a new link or cancel old links at any time.

Does it track me after the event?

No. Location is shared only during the event, and only your latest spot is kept. On the web it's sent only while the page is open, and only when you've moved. When the event ends, it's deleted along with everything else.

Does it work on iPhone?

Friends can join from Safari on any iPhone. To get notifications on iPhone, add the page to your Home Screen (iOS 16.4 or later). The app for starting events is on Android first.

Why did I see "We've hit today's event limit"?

To keep EventCrew free, there's a limit on how many new events can start each day. If it's reached, you'll see that message and can try again the next day. Events that are already running are never cut off.

Is it free?

Yes. The free plan covers events of up to 15 people and 12 hours, with ads. Pro raises the limits and removes ads for the whole group.
